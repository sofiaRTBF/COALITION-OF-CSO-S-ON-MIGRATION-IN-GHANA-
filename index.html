CCMG goals

What guides us

Vision

To see a world where there is safe, orderly and regular migration while ensuring the protection of the rights of migrants and sustainable economic development in sending, transit and receiving countries.

Mission

To contribute towards migration governance in Ghana and the implementation of the GCM in line with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through advocacy, sensitisation, research, outreach and capacity building.

Guiding principles

In all its actions, the Coalition is guided, inter alia, by the United Nations Charter, international law, the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, its constitution and the GCM. In its working methods it aligns with the terms of reference of the West and Central Africa Regional Network on Migration and the Global UN Network on Migration.

About the coalition

Who we are & what we do

Who are we?

CCMG has a diverse and experienced membership of NGOs working on migration, child protection, anti-trafficking, community development, human rights, livelihoods, advocacy, research and social inclusion. Members work nationally and across the Northern, Volta, Oti, Eastern, Central, Ashanti, Bono, Upper East, Upper West and Greater Accra Regions, giving CCMG real potential to generate grassroots evidence and influence migration policy at national and local level.

18 December 2024

International Migrants Day 2024

CCMG marked the day at Tawala Beach and McDan Astroturf, Labadi, with a wreath-laying ceremony at sea in memory of lives lost on perilous journeys, and a football gala between the Nigerian and Liberian migrant communities. Our thanks to the sponsoring members, the European Union, government officials, migrant communities and everyone who took part.

Focus project

AU Free Movement Protocol advocacy

With the Coalition of CSOs on Migration in Africa, CCMG is promoting ratification and implementation of the African Union Free Movement Protocol across 11 African Member States, through training, national dialogue and public screenings.
